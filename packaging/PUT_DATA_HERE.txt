No imaging data is distributed with this repository, and none will be.

The cohort is retrospective human-subjects CT acquired at the National Institutes
of Health under an approved institutional review board protocol. It is not ours to
redistribute, and access is governed by that protocol rather than by this
repository. Please do not open issues requesting it.

This directory exists so that the pipeline has a documented place to look if you
hold an equivalently structured cohort of your own.

Expected layout, relative to the project root:

  detection_data_NIH/original/Slices/{CLASS}_{ID}/{ID}_{STUDY}_Slice_{N}.tiff
  detection_data_NIH/original/XMLs/  {CLASS}_{ID}/{ID}_{STUDY}_Slice_{N}.xml

where {CLASS} is one of SDHx, VHL_EPAS1, Kinase, Sporadic. Slices are 8-bit
pre-windowed axial venous-phase TIFFs; each XML carries the lesion bounding
boxes for its slice. There are no segmentation contours.

To use a copy that lives elsewhere, either symlink it:

  ln -s /path/to/your/cohort detection_data_NIH

or point the config at it and leave the data where it is:

  ./run_all.sh --set data.root=/path/to/your/cohort/original

Only original/ is read. If your layout has sibling train/ and test/ directories,
they are ignored on purpose: a flat split that drops the class prefix cannot
distinguish two patients who share a numeric ID, and numeric IDs are reused
across classes in this cohort (84 collisions). Splitting on the bare number
merges distinct patients and leaks them between training and test. The patient
key is therefore {CLASS}_{ID}, never {ID} alone.
